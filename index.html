About

I came to software engineering through mechanical engineering. During my PhD at Utah State University, I studied how uncertainty in electricity prices shapes the way we design and operate integrated energy systems. I finished my doctorate in 2024 and joined Idaho National Laboratory as a modeling and simulation researcher.

At INL, I develop software to help researchers decide what generating capacity to build, how reliably it will meet demand, and whether projects make economic sense under different assumptions. My work spans high-performance simulation, web apps for exploring results, and AI agent tools for research workflows.
